About EverHaven

Built to bridge creativity and manufacturing.

Most good ideas die in the gap between a sketch and a finished object. EverHaven exists to close that gap — a studio that designs and makes its own products, and brings the same process to the parts and products our customers need.

A studio, not a portal: when you work with us, you're talking to the people who design and make your parts.
