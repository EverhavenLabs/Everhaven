03 · Space, grid, radius, motion

Room to breathe.

Grid

12 columns · 1200px max · 24px gutters. Hero 6/6 · features 3-up · paths 2-up · process 4-up. Sections full-bleed, content contained.

Spacing

4px base: 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 24 / 32 / 48 / 64 / 112. Section rhythm 112px desktop, 64px mobile. Card interior 32px. When in doubt, add space.

Radius & elevation

6px buttons/inputs · 10px cards · 14px hero artifacts · pill badges. One shadow only — 0 1px 3px rgba(22,24,26,.08) — for overlays and card hover. Everything else is flat + hairline.

Motion

Fades and ≤12px translates. 200ms ease-out hover · 300ms ease-in-out layout. One reveal per section. Nothing scales or moves on hover. Honors reduced-motion.

04 · Photography

Three shots. One grade.

Every image on the site is one of three types, warm-neutral graded. Until real photography exists, frames carry their shot brief in the spec layer — drop real images straight onto them.

01 · Macro detail

Surface textures, tolerances made visible. Proof of precision.

02 · Object on set

Products on warm-neutral seamless. Same lighting across the whole catalog.

03 · Environment

The workshop in motion, shallow depth of field. The human layer.

Never: stock offices, handshakes, cool grading, b&w, HDR, watermarked renders. Empty frames sit on the surface tone with their brief — they are part of the design, not a gap in it.

05 · Iconography

Stroke icons, ink only.

Lucide set, 1.5px stroke, 20/24px. Ink or muted — never the signal color, so icons never compete with actions. No emoji, anywhere.
