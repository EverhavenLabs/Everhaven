Capabilities · FDM 3D printing

FDM 3D printing.

Fused deposition modeling builds parts layer by layer from thermoplastic — the process behind everything we print, in PLA and PETG.

When it's the right fit

Functional parts, prototypes, replacement pieces, enclosures, brackets, fixtures, and original products — especially where iteration speed and per-part cost matter more than injection-molded finish.

Honest limitations

Layer lines are visible; strength varies with print orientation; very fine detail and some geometries suit other processes better. We assess fit per project — and say so when FDM isn't the answer.

Typical use cases

Prototype iterations · replacement parts · small-batch product runs · fixtures and organizers · enclosures and mounts. EXPAND WITH REAL PROJECTS

What you can provide

A file, image, sketch, drawing, or reference — file preparation and design support cover the gap between what you have and what printing needs.
